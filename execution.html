Proposed progress measures

If approved, progress could be reported monthly against five proposed indicators: (1) surveillance site uptime (target ≥95%); (2) alert-to-verification time (target ≤48h); (3) prototype family milestone completion; (4) bio-foundry validation batch pass rate; and (5) budget performance against the approved baseline. Define each measure, baseline, data owner, and tolerance during planning. Share public summaries only after privacy, security, and partner review. A variance trigger and recovery process require approval.

Proposed people and engagement approach

If partners proceed, the people plan should connect work to community outcomes, recognize contributions appropriately, and define training and career pathways. These practices should be developed with participating employers and staff rather than assumed in advance.

Supporting workforce wellbeing

The delivery plan defines staffing and professional development, but does not yet describe wellbeing support during surge periods. Consider planning protected rest, access to mental-health resources, and healthy meals for extended shifts. These are recommendations for planning, not approved scope or budget commitments.
