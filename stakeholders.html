Proposed institutional champion: UFV

This proposal identifies the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) as a potential institutional champion because of its regional presence and possible research and workforce-development role. UFV’s interest, capacity, and willingness to participate have not been confirmed. A champion, governance structure, and funding authority must be agreed before the project can be initiated.

Handling stakeholders: a power/interest grid

High power / High interest: Manage closely

Potential partners include Fraser Health, BCCDC, UFV, the First Nations Health Authority, and affected First Nations. Decision rights and engagement protocols must be established with each party; no agreement is presumed.

High power / Low interest: Keep satisfied

Provincial Ministry of Health. They do not need daily detail, but they must never be surprised by a scope or budget variance.

Low power / High interest: Keep informed

School districts, community groups, and the public. Early, accessible engagement is proposed; requirements and channels should be developed with affected communities.

Low power / Low interest: Monitor

Peripheral vendors and general media. Standard procurement and communication channels suffice.
