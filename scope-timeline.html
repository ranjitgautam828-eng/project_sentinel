PROJECT PROPOSAL / SCOPE & TIME

Fraser Valley Sentinel · $1 billion pandemic preparedness initiative

02 · Scope & Time

What is in scope, what is explicitly out, and when it happens

In Scope

  • Fraser Valley Sentinel Surveillance Network (wastewater + air-monitoring at travel nodes).
  • Access-controlled pathogen data platform; any public summaries subject to privacy, security, and governance approval.
  • Prototype pathogen countermeasures library (≥10 viral families).
  • One modular mRNA bio-foundry in the Fraser Valley.
  • Far-UVC clean-air retrofits in 50+ high-risk rooms (schools, hospitals, transit).
  • At-home molecular test subsidy and UFV validation lab.
  • Workforce development pipeline through UFV.

Out of Scope

  • Acute-care hospital construction or new bed capacity.
  • Emergency healthcare staffing or surge hiring.
  • Mass PPE stockpiles (a government-scale procurement function).
  • National or international vaccine purchasing.
  • Regulatory approval of specific products (partners lead this).
  • Long-term operation after the proposed close; a future operator and funding source remain to be agreed.

Scope boundary: This proposal focuses mainlyon preparedness and earlier warning; it does not replace acute-care capacity. The proposed $1 billion envelope, scope, and any prevention benefits require a proper feasibility analysis and formal approval.

A monitoring signal would not automatically trigger research, manufacturing, or deployment. As those decisions require a separate scientific, public-health, regulatory, and funding approvals.

Proposed performance targets for feasibility testing: 48 hours from a novel signal to a verified alert; 21 days from sequence confirmation to a validated local batch for a pathogen that matches an existing prototype family. But neither target is validated or guaranteed.
