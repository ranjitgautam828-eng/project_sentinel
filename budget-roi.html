Economic rationale and scenario evidence

This is not a conventional revenue-generating project. The references below provide context for a future benefits analysis; they do not establish a return on this proposed investment. Three evidence areas are relevant.

1. Wastewater lead time

In an Ontario study model, a wastewater program costing $15M/year was cost-effective in scenarios with at least three days of earlier detection and cost-saving in scenarios with ten or more days of lead time (Government of Canada, 2026). Those results do not demonstrate that this proposal would achieve the same lead time or economics.

2. Avoided hospital burden

In one modeled Ontario Omicron scenario, ten-day-earlier detection was associated with an estimated 6,345 hospitalizations, 1,459 ICU admissions, and 702 deaths avoided per wave (Government of Canada, 2026). These are not Fraser Health forecasts and cannot be used directly to claim local savings.

3. The shutdown benchmark

Canada’s first two years of COVID-19 losses were approximately $242B (Cotton, 2026). A $1B proposed portfolio is roughly 0.4% of that historical loss. This ratio is context only. It is not a payback threshold, a savings estimate, or evidence that a shutdown would be avoided.

Cost of quality: Validation, testing, and redundancy must be estimated within the work-package budgets. No separate quality or contingency reserve is identified in this draft. The cost of a missed signal or failed batch is a risk to assess. This proposal does not quantify those savings.

Cost-risk analysis required before approval

Historical public-works research reports frequent cost overruns, including an 86% overrun rate in its reference data (Flyvbjerg et al., 2002). This is a warning to develop a project-specific estimate, not a calibrated probability or contingency recommendation for this program.

This draft does not include a project-specific Monte Carlo model, P50/P80 estimate, or separately sized contingency. Before a funding decision, develop work-package estimates, document assumptions and schedule dependencies, quantify uncertainty, and present a risk-adjusted funding range. The current $1B allocation has no unallocated reserve.
