Decision requested

Authorize a time-limited feasibility and detailed-planning phase only. This does not authorize full implementation of the $1 billion scenario.

  • Validating technical, operational, regulatory, privacy, and site feasibility.
  • Confirming a willing sponsor and negotiating partner roles; no organization is presumed to have agreed.
  • Preparing independently reviewed work-package estimates, a contingency reserve, funding plan, and benefits case.

Any implementation, procurement, or release of the proposed program budget would require separate approval.

THE STRATEGY

Three capabilities. One coordinated response.

The proposal prioritizes speed: recognize a threat earlier, prepare countermeasures sooner, and make essential products closer to home.
